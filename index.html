About this app

InstaSaver is a lightweight, open-source Android app to download Instagram Reels, posts and profile pictures in original quality — no login required, no watermarks, no tracking.

  • Download Reels, Posts & Profile Pictures in HD quality
  • No account / login needed — works with public content only
  • Built with Kotlin + MVVM + Material Design
  • Zero ads, zero analytics, zero data collection
  • Saves directly to your Downloads folder

Disclaimer: Use only for content you own or have permission to download. Respect Instagram's Terms of Service.
